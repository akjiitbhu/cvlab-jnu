Dr. Ankit Kumar Jaiswal

Assistant Professor, Computer Science and Engineering, School of Engineering, Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), New Delhi.

ankitjaiswal@jnu.ac.in Google Scholar ORCID LinkedIn

Source: Google Scholar · as of —

Dr. Jaiswal joined the School of Engineering at JNU in March 2023. His doctoral work at IIT (BHU) Varanasi addressed the detection and localization of tampered regions in digital photographs, combining statistical forensic cues with deep segmentation models — a line of work that produced a granted Indian patent and a series of journal papers on splicing and copy-move forgery.

His current interests extend that foundation in two directions: towards synthetic media, where the question is no longer which part of a photograph was edited but whether it was ever captured at all, and towards applied vision, where the same representation learning supports glaucoma screening, histopathology analysis, and person re-identification.

Alongside research he teaches core computer science to undergraduates — programming, data structures, and databases — as well as digital image processing, and coordinates examinations, M.Tech. dissertation allocation, and the school's digital presence.
